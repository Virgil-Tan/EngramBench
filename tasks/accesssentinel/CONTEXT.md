# AccessSentinel Context

AccessSentinel is a privileged-access control plane, not a general identity provider or policy authoring toy.

- Session and Device Trust establish who and what is asking.
- Policy and Risk revisions define immutable decision inputs.
- Location observations add ordered regional risk evidence.
- Access Requests freeze authority; Risk Decisions and Reviews decide; Grants are exact and short lived.
- Revocation fences override every normal grant path and stale cache.
- Audit and transactional Events make every security transition attributable and recoverable.

The benchmark observes only published HTTP, process, seed, snapshot, browser, and project-command seams.
